# CampusMate demo script (about 90 seconds)

1. Start at the Assistant page and point out the local-data notice. Explain that Alderwick College is fictional and the app does not connect to live campus systems.
2. Click **Find a classroom**. Show how CampusMate returns the building, floor, room, nearby landmark, facilities, hours, timetable note, and contact for C-204.
3. Ask **Where is the AI Lab?** Show the lab location, capacity, installed systems, hours, booking instructions, and contact.
4. Ask **What events are happening today?** The displayed events match today's date in the local sample dataset. If there are no events for today's date, CampusMate says so rather than inventing one.
5. Ask **When does the library close?** Show the Central Library hours and services.
6. Ask **How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?** Show the office, location, steps, documents, hours, and contact.
7. Ask **Which classrooms are free now?** Explain that the sample has no live timetable, so CampusMate cannot confirm real-time availability.
8. Ask an unrelated question, such as **What is the price of a flight to Delhi?** Show the fallback and supported topics.
9. Open **About this demo**, then the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages. Close by noting that query matching is local and chat history is session-only.
